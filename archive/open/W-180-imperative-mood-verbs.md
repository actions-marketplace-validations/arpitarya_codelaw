## Closed 2026-11-18

- **Table (DoD 1):** `packs/python/data/python/imperatives.json`, generated by `scripts/data-imperatives.py` from pydocstyle 6.3.0's sdist (SHA-256 pinned, checked before a byte is read); `--check` regenerates and compares, in CI's lint job (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`). **An in-pack table, not a data package:** L6 decision 15's data packages are for tables that change on the world's schedule; this one is a pinned snapshot, so the mechanism is the spell pack's (`spell-data.py`, D-42 to D-44). Reasoning in SR-RULE-PACKS decision 20.
- **L11 (DoD 2):** row D-56 (source, MIT, pin). **Taken with the verb list: the blacklist** (`imperatives_blacklist.txt`, the same package, version and licence), because Ruff's D401 reads both; the row says to strike `blacklist` if the ratification meant the verb list only.
- **Rules (DoD 3):** `py/non-imperative-mood` (D401) and `py/property-docstring-starts-with-verb` (D421), `preview`, `manual` (`authored`), in `py/pydocstyle`, with cases.
- **Stemmer:** written fresh (`stem` in `crates/codelaw-native/src/py_imperative.rs`, Snowball English algorithm), no library; 0 of 240,571 words differ from `snowballstemmer` 2.2.0 (a measuring instrument in a scratch venv, not a dependency).
- **One thing the item assumed that is not so:** Ruff's **D421 does not read the verb table**: it has its own ten verbs (`return`, `get`, `yield`, `fetch`, `retrieve` and their `-s` forms). The rule follows Ruff.
